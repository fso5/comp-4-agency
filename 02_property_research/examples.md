## Example 1 — Specific-property deep dive

**Incoming Handoff Packet (from 03 or human, not the typical 01 → 02 flow):**

> Andersons just saw 3411 Garden Villa Ln pop up on Zillow this morning (it's not in our research_brief from last week). They're asking if we can get a deeper read before the showing tomorrow. Maria wants to know: is this one a real contender for them at $735k? What should she watch for on the walkthrough?

```markdown
# Research Brief — 3411 Garden Villa Ln (Mueller)

**Produced by:** 02_property_research
**Date:** 2026-05-12
**Requested by:** Maria, for 5/13 11am showing
**Scope:** specific-property deep dive on 3411 Garden Villa Ln

## TL;DR
3411 Garden Villa Ln is a strong fit on paper for the Andersons ($735k, 3/2.5/2010sf, large fenced yard, Mueller). Listed 2 days ago. Three things to verify on the walkthrough: HOA fee (listed as $215 but neighbors report $240 due to upcoming greenway assessment), the walkability to Maplewood (it's on the south edge of Mueller, 0.7mi instead of the typical 0.4mi), and the corner-lot exposure to traffic on Berkman Dr.

## Property facts
- **Address:** 3411 Garden Villa Ln, Austin TX 78723
- **List price:** $735,000 (listed 5/10)
- **Beds/Baths/Sqft:** 3/2.5/2010 (MLS) / 1988 (TCAD — note 22sf discrepancy, MLS likely includes a converted bonus space)
- **Lot size:** 0.14 acres (TCAD)
- **Year built:** 2014
- **HOA:** Mueller Owners Association, listed $215/mo
- **MLS days on market:** 2
- **Source:** ABoR MLS pull 5/12 7am, TCAD record verified

## Recent comp context
- 4520 Aldrich St (sold 4/22 at $725k, 4/2.5/2120sf) — similar build, sold slightly above list
- 4011 Mueller Blvd (sold 3/30 at $688k, 3/2.5/1850sf) — smaller, sold at list
- 1247 Berkman Dr (active, $719k, 4/2.5/2080sf) — Anderson's other top contender; better house IMO but worth comparison

3411 Garden Villa list price is consistent with the comp set. Not a steal, not overpriced.

## What to watch for on the showing
1. **Corner lot on Berkman.** Berkman Dr is the secondary thoroughfare through Mueller. Property fronts a quieter street but the long side of the lot is on Berkman. Look at fence height, noise from yard, sun exposure on the bedroom side.
2. **HOA assessment rumor.** Pulled the last 12 months of Mueller HOA meeting minutes — there's a vote upcoming on a greenway-maintenance special assessment. Could add $25/mo. Worth Maria asking listing agent directly.
3. **Walk to Maplewood Elementary.** 0.7mi, mostly along Berkman. Crossable but not the protected 0.4mi walk to school the Andersons are imagining if they were thinking Mueller-center.
4. **Disclosure:** Seller's Disclosure Notice was uploaded with the MLS listing. Worth scanning — nothing flagged in the version available, but verify with listing agent that nothing's been amended since.
5. **Property tax history:** assessed at $14,200 in 2025; expect a re-assessment after sale ($16-17k more likely at the new sale price). Travis County tax bills are October.

## Recommended action
Maria — this is a real contender but not a clear win over 1247 Berkman. If the Andersons saw both on Saturday, lean into the comparison. If they're only going to see one, 1247 Berkman is more centrally located and (in my read) better for Pat's walkability rule.

## Sources

- TCAD record for 3411 Garden Villa Ln (parcel #...)

- WalkScore.com for distance verification
- Diana Lin Realty case archive (4520 Aldrich St case 2025-12-aldrich-buyer, sold 4/22)
```

## Example 3 — Flag back to human (budget doesn't support expectation)

**Incoming Handoff Packet (from 01):**

> New qualified buyer — Sherman couple. Stated budget $475-525k. Stated target: Tarrytown, Clarksville, Old West Austin. Score: warm.

**My research_brief.md (abbreviated, but the routing is the headline):**

```markdown
# Research Brief — Sherman (Buyer)

**Produced by:** 02_property_research
**Date:** 2026-05-14
**Requested by:** Tom, for first discovery call

## TL;DR — please read before researching further

The Shermans' stated budget of $475-525k cannot buy a detached SFH meeting their stated property type (3+ bed, 2+ bath, 1500+ sqft, move-in ready) in Tarrytown, Clarksville, or Old West Austin. **There are zero active listings meeting all criteria in those neighborhoods today.** Lowest detached SFH active in those neighborhoods is $785k (3/2/1680, Clarksville).

**Tom, this is a budget-vs-expectation conversation, not a research conversation.** I'm pausing the full brief and flagging back to you. Three options to discuss with the Shermans:
1. **Same budget, different neighborhood.** $475-525k buys nicely in Allandale, Brentwood, or North Loop. I can produce a brief for those.
2. **Same neighborhood, different property type.** $475-525k can buy a condo or small townhome in Tarrytown/Clarksville. Some are 2/2/1100sf in good condition.
3. **Wait or expand budget.** Tarrytown SFH inventory is rare and pricey; $750k+ floor.

## Sources for the disqualification
- ABoR MLS pull 5/14 11am: filtered Tarrytown/Clarksville/OWA detached SFH 1500+ sqft 3+ bed, $475-525k → 0 results
- Lowest qualifying detached in those neighborhoods last 90 days sold for $735k (2018 Pecos St, sold 2/14/26)

## Routing recommendation
→ **human:Tom** for the budget reset conversation. I can produce a full brief once expectations are aligned.
```

## Pattern notes

- **The brief format flexes.** Standard neighborhood brief ≠ specific-property deep dive ≠ CMA. Pick the shape that fits the question.
- **TL;DR is sacred.** It must be the part the agent reads first, and it must contain enough to act.
- **"What to flag" is the difference between a research dump and a usable brief.** Always end on a recommendation, even if the recommendation is "stop and talk to the human."
- **Source everything.** "Where did you get $385/sqft?" must have an answer in the brief itself, not in my head.
