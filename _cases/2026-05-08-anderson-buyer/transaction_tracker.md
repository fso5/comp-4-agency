## Texas-specific notes

- **Option period** is unique to Texas and runs concurrent with other contingencies. During it, buyer can terminate for any reason for the option fee paid (here, $250).
- **Mineral rights** — not retained by seller per contract paragraph 2.A.4. Confirmed.
- **Property tax estimate** — Travis County. 2025 tax assessed at $14,800. Buyer should plan ~$1,235/mo escrow.
- **HOA** — Mueller's HOA includes greenway/park maintenance. $215/mo, paid quarterly. Resale certificate fee is seller's responsibility per Mueller standard.
- **TREC promulgated forms** used throughout. Standard 1-4 Family Resale Residential Contract. Use TXR addendums where applicable (HOA, Seller's Temporary Lease if requested, etc.).
- **Closing Disclosure (CD) 3-day rule** — federal TRID rule. Buyer must receive CD at least 3 business days before close. Any change to APR, loan product, or prepayment penalty resets the clock.
