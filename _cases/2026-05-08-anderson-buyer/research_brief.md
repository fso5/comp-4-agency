## Neighborhood briefs

### Mueller

**Character.** Master-planned redevelopment of old Austin airport (closed 1999). Tight street grid, mixed housing types, big greenway through the middle, walkable to Mueller H-E-B and Aldrich Street retail. Very dog-friendly — leash culture is real. Demographic skews young families + remote-work professionals. Pat will feel at home here.

**Walkability.** Mueller's WalkScore averages 75 for the residential streets, 85+ near Aldrich. The Andersons can walk to: H-E-B, Halcyon coffee, Thunderbird Coffee, B.D. Riley's, Alamo Drafthouse Mueller, Mueller Lake Park (15 acres + dog area), the Thinkery (kids' museum). Bus rapid transit on Berkman Dr.

**Schools (AISD).**
- Elementary: Maplewood (rated B+, source: Austin ISD report card + GreatSchools 7/10)
- Middle: Lamar (rated B, GreatSchools 6/10)
- High: Northeast (rated C+, GreatSchools 5/10) — Pat may want to investigate if 7–10 year horizon includes high-school-age kids
- *For a teacher: Maplewood is 0.5 mi from Mueller center — Pat could realistically walk to work if hired there.*

**Inventory snapshot (pulled 5/9 morning).**
- 12 active SFH in $650–750k range
- 8 of those 3/2+ at 1700+ sqft
- Median days on market: 9 days for this price band in last 30 days
- Avg sold-to-list ratio last 30 days: 99.2%
- Median sold price/sqft: $385

**Sources:** Austin Board of Realtors MLS (5/9 7am pull), GreatSchools.org, AISD report cards 2024–25, WalkScore.com.

### Holly

**Character.** East-of-35 neighborhood adjacent to Lady Bird Lake. Less manicured than Mueller, more "old East Austin gentrifying" — older bungalows mixed with infill new builds. Strong walkability to Cesar Chavez restaurant strip and the lake trail, but the southern edge of Holly fronts a busy commercial corridor.

**Walkability.** WalkScore 72 average. Walk to Justine's, Veracruz tacos, Hillside Farmacy, the hike-and-bike trail. Less internal retail than Mueller.

**Schools (AISD).**
- Elementary: Metz (GreatSchools 6/10)
- Middle: Martin (GreatSchools 5/10)
- High: Eastside Memorial (GreatSchools 4/10)
- Generally Mueller schools rate better. Not disqualifying, but worth Maria mentioning.

**Inventory snapshot.**
- 7 active SFH in $650–750k
- Median days on market: 14 days
- Avg sold-to-list ratio last 30 days: 98.1%
- Median sold price/sqft: $415 (higher than Mueller because lot sizes are smaller and condition varies)

**Watch for:** properties on E Cesar Chavez itself — Pat's "no major thoroughfare" rule. Also some Holly listings are on busy 7th St.

**Sources:** ABoR MLS, GreatSchools.org, WalkScore.com.

---

### East Cesar Chavez

**Character.** Smaller, hipper, denser. More townhouses and modern infill, fewer traditional yards. Trendy restaurants, music venues, art studios. Active nightlife edge along Cesar Chavez and 6th Street East.

**Walkability.** WalkScore 78 (highest of the three for some pockets). Walk to most of Rainey Street, the lake, downtown bridges.

**Schools.** Same as Holly (Metz / Martin / Eastside).

**Inventory snapshot.**
- 4 active SFH in $650–750k (most stock here is townhomes which Pat ruled out)
- Smallest yards of the three neighborhoods
- Average sqft on detached homes in band: 1,500 (smaller than Andersons want)

**My honest read:** probably not the right fit. Pat wants a yard and 1700+ sqft, and East Cesar Chavez delivers smaller and denser. Worth one showing for context if Maria has time, but I'd weight this last.

**Sources:** ABoR MLS, WalkScore.com, in-person walk-throughs by team (Tom's notes from 4/24 buyer trip).
