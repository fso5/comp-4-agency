# Austin Market Reference

Background context that 02_property_research relies on, that 03 uses for color, and that any team member can lean on for neighborhood-level talking points. Not a substitute for live MLS data — but a steady reference for "what is this neighborhood actually like."

**Refresh cadence:** quarterly. Or when a major event (school district news, light-rail expansion, big employer move) changes the landscape.

## Neighborhoods we work in regularly

Listed by geographic area. Coverage: roughly central Austin within 5 miles of downtown, plus select adjacent areas. Outside this footprint, route to a referral via `human:Diana`.

### Mueller / North-East-Central

**Boundaries.** Roughly Berkman Dr (E/W) and Manor Rd to E 51st St (N/S). Plus the post-2010 Mueller redevelopment district (former Mueller airport site).

**Character.** Master-planned, walkable, family-leaning. Tight street grid, mid-density. Mueller proper is the most "designed-from-scratch" feel in Austin — clean, walkable, intentional. Strong sense of community.

**Walkability.** WalkScore 70–85 in the residential pockets; 85+ near Aldrich Street retail and the Mueller H-E-B.

**Schools (AISD zones — verify per-address).**
- Elementary: Maplewood (B+, GreatSchools 7/10)
- Middle: Lamar (B, GreatSchools 6/10)
- High: Northeast (C+, GreatSchools 5/10) — worth flagging to long-horizon families

**Price band.** SFH detached typically $650–950k. New-builds in the planned section can hit $1.2M+. Condos/townhomes $400-650k.

**Days on market (active band).** 8–14 typical. Faster than Austin avg.

**Notable.** Mueller Lake Park (15 acres, dog area), Thinkery (kids' museum), Alamo Drafthouse Mueller, Halcyon coffee. Easy access to 35 + 290.

**Watch for.** Mueller HOA fees (~$215/mo standard, with occasional special assessments). Property tax bills run high because Travis County reassesses fast in this area.

### Holly / East Cesar Chavez / East Austin (south of MLK)

**Boundaries.** Holly is roughly E 7th to Ladybird Lake, IH-35 east to Pleasant Valley. East Cesar Chavez is along the Cesar Chavez St corridor, east of IH-35.

**Character.** Older East Austin, mixed bungalows and infill new builds, trending more expensive each year. Walkable to the lake trail. Restaurants and bars are a defining feature.

**Walkability.** WalkScore 72–78. Walk to Justine's, Veracruz tacos, Hillside Farmacy, the hike-and-bike trail.

**Schools (AISD).** Metz Elementary (6/10), Martin Middle (5/10), Eastside Memorial High (4/10). Generally lower than Mueller; flag for school-priority families.

**Price band.** SFH $600–900k. Newer infill in the $700–900k range. Renovated bungalows $650–800k.

**Days on market.** 12–18 typical. Slightly slower than Mueller.

**Notable.** Lady Bird Lake trail, Rainey Street nightlife, Cesar Chavez restaurant strip.

**Watch for.** Properties fronting Cesar Chavez or 7th Street can be noisy/traffic-heavy. Tax assessments rising fast as gentrification continues — buyers should plan for tax escalation.

### Travis Heights / South Congress / Bouldin

**Boundaries.** Travis Heights: 78704, north of Oltorf, east of South Congress. Bouldin: similar but west of South Congress.

**Character.** Historic, leafy, expensive. Older homes (1920s–40s) with mature oaks. Walking distance to South Congress retail and downtown bridges. Old-Austin energy.

**Walkability.** WalkScore 70–82. Stacy Park, Big Stacy pool, the SoCo walk.

**Schools.** Travis Heights Elementary (8/10), Lively Middle (B+), Travis High (C+). Strong elementary; high school is hit-or-miss.

**Price band.** SFH $900k–$2.5M. Renovated craftsmans clear $1.2M routinely. Original-condition bungalows in the $850-1M range as fixer-uppers.

**Days on market.** 14–24 typical. Slower in this band; buyers and sellers both pickier.

**Notable.** Mature oaks, hilly terrain (good views in spots), Stacy Park, walk to SoCo, easy downtown access via Congress bridge.

**Watch for.** Older homes — verify electrical (knob-and-tube risk in pre-1950s), foundation (clay soil shifts), plumbing (cast iron). Always recommend pre-inspection for sellers in this band.

### Allandale / Brentwood / North Loop

**Boundaries.** Allandale north of 45th, west of Burnet. Brentwood east of Burnet. North Loop is the small pocket south of Allandale around 51st.

**Character.** Mid-century, mostly single-story homes from the 1950s-60s. Quiet, family-friendly. Less manicured than Mueller, more affordable than Travis Heights.

**Walkability.** WalkScore 55–70. More car-dependent than central neighborhoods. Some retail on Burnet.

**Schools (AISD).** Gullett (8/10), Lamar (6/10), McCallum (B+, well-regarded high school).

**Price band.** SFH $550–850k. Updated mid-century clears $750k. Unrenovated $550–650k.

**Days on market.** 12–18 typical.

**Notable.** McCallum High is the AISD performing arts magnet — draws families. Walkable to Top Notch Hamburgers, Quack's, and the Burnet retail strip.

**Watch for.** Many mid-century homes still on slab with original 1960s plumbing. Renovations may be needed; budget accordingly. Older HVAC systems common.

### Tarrytown / Old West Austin / Clarksville

**Boundaries.** West of MoPac, north of the lake, south of 35th.

**Character.** Old money, expensive, established. Some of the prettiest streets in Austin. Mature trees, lake views in spots.

**Walkability.** WalkScore varies — 45 in Tarrytown proper, 75 in Clarksville.

**Schools.** Casis Elementary (10/10, top-rated AISD), O Henry Middle (B+), Austin High (B+). Best public-school pocket in town.

**Price band.** SFH $1.2M – $5M+. Tarrytown floor is high.

**Days on market.** 18–30 typical. Slower band; buyers patient.

**Watch for.** Inventory is often outside our service tier. Below $1M in these neighborhoods means a condo or a true fixer.

---

### Bouldin / Zilker / Barton Hills

**Boundaries.** South of the lake, west of South Lamar.

**Character.** Trendy, walkable to the lake and Barton Springs. Older homes mixed with new builds. ACL festival territory.

**Walkability.** 65–75 WalkScore. Lake trail, Barton Springs, ZACH theater.

**Schools.** Zilker Elementary (8/10), O Henry (B+), Austin High (B+).

**Price band.** $850k – $2M.

**Days on market.** 14–22 typical.

## Data sources we trust

For property research, use these in preference order:

1. **Austin Board of Realtors MLS (ABoR)** — sold and active listings. Authoritative.
2. **TCAD (Travis County Appraisal District)** — sqft, lot size, year built, ownership, tax assessed value. Authoritative for tax records.
3. **AISD Report Cards** — official school ratings. Cross-check with GreatSchools.
4. **GreatSchools.org** — secondary school ratings + parent reviews.
5. **WalkScore.com** — walkability scores.
6. **Travis County Clerk records** — deed history, liens, easements.
7. **Diana Lin Realty case archive** — internal comp knowledge from past deals.

Avoid: Zillow's Zestimate, Redfin's estimate, generic "national" data sources, AI-generated comp predictions without underlying sales data. If 02 cites one of these in a brief, return for revision.

## School-rating cross-reference

GreatSchools 1–10 and AISD report-card letter grades correlate roughly:

| GreatSchools | AISD letter (approx) | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| 9–10 | A+ to A | Excellent — selling point |
| 7–8 | A- to B+ | Strong — works for most families |
| 5–6 | B to B- | Average — flag to school-priority buyers |
| 3–4 | C+ to C | Below average — must flag |
| 1–2 | C- and below | Significant concern — discuss honestly |

Always quote the actual AISD report card grade, not just GreatSchools. AISD is the authoritative local source.

## Property tax cadence (Travis County)

- **Assessment year:** January 1
- **Notice of appraised value:** typically mailed early April
- **Protest deadline:** May 15 (this year), or 30 days after notice — whichever is later
- **Tax bills issued:** October
- **Payment due:** January 31 (without penalty)

When estimating monthly tax escrow for a buyer:
- Travis County combined rate is around 1.9–2.2% of assessed value (school + city + county + ACC + healthcare district)
- Take the assessed value × rate / 12 = monthly escrow estimate
- For a $700k home, expect $1,100–1,300/mo in tax escrow

| Travis Heights | Usually none (no HOA) | A few small co-ops or condo conversions have HOAs |
| Holly / East Austin | Usually none | New townhome developments have HOAs $150-250/mo |
| Allandale / Brentwood | Usually none | Established SFH neighborhoods, no HOA |
